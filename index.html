Pure, Clean-Label Confectionery

The Master Ingredient Deck

Traditional wagashi has been celebrated for centuries as a clean, simple, and naturally clean-label luxury. Unlike commercial Western sweets that rely on chemical emulsifiers, we use only time-honored methods.

  • Premium Shiro-An (White Bean Paste): Rinsed and pureed butter beans sweetened with organic cane sugar. Smooth, clean, and velvety.
  • Gyuhi Paste (Glutinous Rice): Sweet rice flour (mochiko) kneaded until shiny and beautifully translucent, granting elastic pillowy chewiness.
  • Steam-Distilled Rose & Local Yuzu: Real zested yuzu grown in Southern California paired with natural organic flower hydrosols.

Naturally Gluten-Free

We utilize 100% pure mochiko sweet rice flour. No gluten stabilizer traces whatsoever.
